Instructions: Introduction to the Gamut Gallery

This course uses a tool called the Gamut Gallery, which you will engage with in the next course item.

The Gamut Gallery facilitates the sharing of digital artifacts with peers and supports discussions around them. Read over the sections below to orient yourself to the Gamut Gallery and its capabilities.

What is the Gamut Gallery?

The Gamut Gallery provides a digital place to submit and share digital artifacts (like images or PDFs), receive feedback, and pose questions to other learners about their submissions. In the course sequence, the Gallery exercises will be titled Share: Personal Core Story, Share: Change Resistance Reflection, and Share: 21 Day Plan.

How does this course use Gamut Gallery?

You'll upload your Core Story in the Gallery. You will also comment on your peers' stories.

How can I get the most out of the Gamut Gallery?

The most valuable way to engage with the Gamut Gallery is to respond thoughtfully and thoroughly to the provided prompts. Upload an artifact that aligns with the instructor’s prompt and explain your rationale for what you shared. After submitting your work, take time to review the submissions of your peers. Click on submissions that you find interesting and leave feedback for the submitter, sharing with them any thoughts that come to mind, questions you had, or feedback they should consider. 

How is my privacy ensured when I use the Gamut Gallery?

When setting up your profile, we will ask for a minimal amount of personal information, such as your name and email address. We will only use this information for academic purposes, such as issuing assignments or assigning grades. You can choose to hide your personal information from your peers within the Gamut Gallery. Doing so will change your display name to Anonymous when sharing work.

How do I report a digital accessibility barrier?

Please use the accessibility feedback form to report an issue you encounter that keeps you from accessing or making progress in the course. We are committed to making our tools accessible for all and welcome feedback.
